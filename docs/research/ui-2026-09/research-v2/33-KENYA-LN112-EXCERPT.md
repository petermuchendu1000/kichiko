 SPECIAL ISSUE 3491 Kenya Gazette Supplement No. 163 30th June, 2026 (Legislative Supplement No. 92) LEGAL NOTICE NO. 112 THE GAMBLING CONTROL ACT (No. 14 of 2025) THE GAMBLING CONTROL (CONDUCT OF GAMBLING OPERATIONS) REGULATIONS, 2026 

 (5) A licensee shall not offer or facilitate a prediction market 
through an online gambling platform except with the prior approval of 
the Authority. 
 
(6) A licensee offering a prediction market shall ensure that the 
outcome of an event contract is determined through a transparent, 
verifiable and independent resolution mechanism approved by the 
Authority. 
 
 (7) A licensee shall not offer or facilitate a prediction market 
where the underlying event— 
 
(a) concerns proceedings pending before a court in Kenya; 
(b) involves the physical safety, health or death of an 
identifiable natural person; or 
(c) is contrary to national security, public order or public 
interest. 
 
 (8) A licensee offering a prediction market shall maintain 
records of all event contracts and settlements and shall make such 
records available to the Authority upon request.
