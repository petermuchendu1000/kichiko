Title: 

Live Chat lets you talk with other traders in real time on a specific event or market while things are happening.

Finding Live Chat

- Open an event or market, then tap Live chat .

- Use the chat switcher to move between the live chats that are currently active.

Sending messages

- Type your message and send. Messages can be up to 300 characters .

- Your messages show your Social nickname and profile photo.

When chat is limited

Live Chat availability depends on the event or market:

- A chat may be read-only , so you can follow along but not send messages.

- A chat may not be available yet, or may be temporarily unavailable.

Keeping chat respectful

Our community standards apply in Live Chat too. See the Community Guidelines . If you see hateful or abusive messages, report or block them .
