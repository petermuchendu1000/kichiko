How to deposit

- Select "Add funds"

- Select the "PayPal" option

- Log in to PayPal and authorize the payment

- Enter the amount you want to deposit

- Review the transfer and add funds

Important information

- The minimum deposit amount is $10 .

Your first PayPal deposit is fee-free. After that, fees may vary up to 2%. There is also no withdrawal hold - funds deposited via PayPal are available for withdrawal immediately.

PayPal is available for US users only and may not be available in all states. Your PayPal account must be in the same name as your Kalshi account.
