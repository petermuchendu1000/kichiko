How to deposit with crypto

- Select "Add funds"

- Select the "Crypto" option

- You will be guided through a step-by-step process to complete your crypto transfer:

- Select one of the supported assets you want to transfer

- Select the network you will transfer on

- Confirm you are sending the correct asset on your desired network

- Initiate the deposit from your wallet using the provided QR code or deposit address

- Always verify the asset and network compatibility before initiating a deposit to ensure successful transactions.

Important information

- Crypto deposits may be subject to transfer provider fees and blockchain network fees (gas).

- Crypto deposits are available for both US and international users.

- Please ensure you are only sending a supported asset on a supported network.

- The maximum amount per crypto deposit depends on the provider your account uses. Your limit is shown on the crypto deposit screen before you confirm.

- Transfers typically take up to 30 minutes to be applied to your cash balance.

- Using direct wallet connections instead of QR codes or manual addresses can help prevent errors and ensure asset compatibility.

- Blockchain confirmation times can vary due to network congestion, which may extend the deposit processing time beyond 30 minutes.

US vs international

- US users: Crypto transfers are processed through Zero Hash, which converts crypto to and from fiat. For support, contact [email protected] or see Zero Hash Support .

- International users: Crypto transfers are processed through a separate provider. The deposit flow is the same - follow the on-screen instructions after selecting "Crypto."

- If you encounter issues, contact Kalshi support with your transaction hash and deposit details for assistance.

- Monitor your blockchain transaction status using the transaction hash to track pending deposits and ensure they are confirmed.
