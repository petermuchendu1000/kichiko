Title: 

Linking a debit card to your Kalshi account is straightforward. If you are adding a debit card for the first time, you will be automatically prompted to input your card details when you initiate a debit deposit.

- Log in to your Kalshi account.

- Initiate a deposit - navigate to the "Add Funds" section and select the debit option.

- Enter card details - input your card number, expiration date, CVV, and cardholder name.

- Verify - double-check that all details are correct and that the cardholder name matches your Kalshi account name.

- Complete - your card will be securely saved for this and future transactions.

Important information

- The cardholder name must match the name on your Kalshi account.

- Visa, Mastercard , and Discover debit cards are accepted for deposits. Other card types may also be processed, but fees can vary and your card issuer may apply additional charges such as a cash advance fee. American Express is not supported.

- Debit cards work for both US and international users.

- You can also use Apple Pay or Google Pay as an alternative to entering card details manually.
