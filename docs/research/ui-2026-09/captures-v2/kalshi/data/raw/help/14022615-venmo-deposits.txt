How to deposit

- Select "Add funds"

- Select the "Venmo" option

- Log in to Venmo and authorize the payment

- Enter the amount you want to deposit

- Review the transfer and add funds

Important information

- The minimum deposit amount is $10 .

Your first deposit is fee-free. After that, fees may vary. There is also no withdrawal hold - funds deposited via Venmo are available for withdrawal immediately.

Venmo is available for US users only and may not be available in all states. Your Venmo account must be in the same name as your Kalshi account.
