Title: 

Inner Circle controls who can see your social trading activity that you haven't made public, including positions, trade history, and P&L.

Stop receiving join requests

In the mobile app:

- Go to Profile .

- Tap Settings .

- Scroll to Inner Circle .

- Toggle Active off.

Note: Turning Inner Circle off blocks new requests. It does not automatically remove existing members or clear old pending requests.

Check who is in your Inner Circle

- Open your Profile .

- Tap the Inner Circle / count pill.

- You'll land on Your Inner Circle .

On Your Inner Circle :

- Pending requests appear first, with Accept and Decline actions.

- Current members appear below pending requests. Tap the remove / X button to remove a member or pending request.

How requests work

- Tapping Decline on a request will not add that person.

- A user only becomes a member if you tap Accept on their request or add them manually.

- You'll receive notifications for new requests and accepted requests.

Worried someone was added after you tapped Decline? Open Your Inner Circle and check the members list. If the account appears there, tap the remove / X button to remove them.
