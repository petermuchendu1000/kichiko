Title: 

Wire deposits are available for both US and international users and can be used for large transfers.

How to Send a Wire

Your bank account name MUST MATCH your name on Kalshi.

- Log in to Kalshi → Menu → Account & Security → verify your FULL NAME.

- Wires must come from an account matching your Kalshi account name. If your account is in your name, send the wire from your personal account - not a third party's.

- If your Kalshi account is under your entity's name, send the wire from an account owned by and named after your entity.

Message to Recipient / Memo (REQUIRED)

Log in to Kalshi → Menu → Transfers → Wire transfer → MESSAGE TO RECIPIENT / MEMO. Your code has 9 digits and starts with "kal." Wires without the correct memo code will be delayed or returned.

Can't find "Message to recipient / Memo"? Depending on your bank, it may be labeled as: Note, Description, Special Instructions, or Further Credit.

Important information

- No fees for wire deposits.

- No withdrawal hold - wire-deposited funds are available for withdrawal immediately.

Wires less than $1,000 will NOT be accepted and will be returned.

- Wires must be received (not sent) by Kalshi Klear LLC before 4pm ET (Eastern Time, New York) for same-day attribution.

- Wires that do not include your unique Kalshi memo code will be delayed or returned.

- Wires from a bank account that doesn't match your Kalshi account name will be delayed or returned.

- ACH transfers to the wire-only account will be returned.

What to Do

Missing or incorrect memo code

Email [email protected] with your wire confirmation and Kalshi account details. Wires without the correct memo will be delayed while we manually match them.

Name on bank account does not match Kalshi

The sender name must match your Kalshi account name. If your bank shows a different name format, email [email protected] with documentation.

Wire sent from a trust or entity account

If your Kalshi account is personal, wires from trust or entity accounts cannot be accepted. Send from a personal account matching your name.

Wire sent but funds not appearing

Wires received before 4pm ET (Eastern Time) are typically attributed same-day. If your wire has not appeared after 1 business day, email [email protected] with your FED reference number.

Wire under $1,000

Wires below the $1,000 minimum are automatically returned to the sending bank. This may take 3-5 business days.
